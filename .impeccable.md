## Design Context

### Users

ZephyrTV serves viewers who want to discover, filter, and play movies, series, anime, short dramas, and live content across desktop and mobile layouts. The interface should support fast scanning and confident playback decisions while keeping returning viewers oriented.

### Brand Personality

Welcoming, energetic, and dependable. The experience should feel contemporary and media-focused without competing with the content itself.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a clear emerald, teal, and sky-blue visual direction that extends the existing green ZephyrTV identity into a blue-leaning gradient. Keep layouts cinematic but readable, with restrained surfaces, strong contrast, and responsive spacing. Avoid purple/pink brand gradients and preserve red, orange, and other semantic colors for status or category meaning.

### Design Principles

- Prioritize content discovery and quick comparison over decoration.
- Use emerald-to-blue gradients for brand surfaces, primary actions, and selected states.
- Preserve semantic colors so errors, warnings, and content categories remain recognizable.
- Maintain readable contrast over imagery with stable fallbacks and layered overlays.
- Keep interactions responsive and consistent across desktop, mobile, touch, and keyboard use.
